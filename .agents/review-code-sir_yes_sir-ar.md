## 1. Historical Commit Audit (`results/sir_yes_sir/`)

All 14 directories under `results/sir_yes_sir/` (8 with `"variant": "ar"`, 6 with `"variant": "sm_mh"`) carry `OUTDATED: built against draft spec` in their `README.md`, conforming to [results.md](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/.agents/results.md#L13-L19). No study in `studies/sir_yes_sir/*.json` cites any of these runs.

Inspecting `git show <sha>:...` across all three `ar` commit SHAs (`1eb53a1de69b17b8a95de3a2a2a4602214c36d81`, `86e46ea99b000dd502c08806c2969ebc906fa824`, and `586f44d05aef4961c3a85a90e4d86e6e525f8eae`) confirmed that `experiments/sir_yes_sir/` and `yessir/` were byte-identical across those three commits, and differed substantially from `HEAD` (`bc2244b`, following commits `89350f7` and `2db3181`):

1. **Task & target trace (`3L` triplets vs `L + 1` symbols + `[EOS]`)**: At `586f44d`, `reference_step` returned a 3-tuple `(o_k, w_k, flag_k)` including an explicit wrap-around indicator `w_k` (`s_1` if `i == B - 1` else `s_0`), and `ar` supervised `3L` tokens per problem ordered by `SirYesSirArConfig.step_order` (`out_first` vs `wrap_first`), terminating on `FLAG_FINISHED`. At `HEAD`, `w_k` and `step_order` are removed; `ar` supervises `L + 1` tokens `(o_0, ..., o_{L-1}, EOS)`.
2. **Output `position_ids` (unaligned `0 .. m - 1` vs position-coupled `1 .. k`)**: At `586f44d`, `sections_to_example` assigned `range(len(tokens))` (`0, 1, ...`) to every section including `TokenType.OUTPUT`, so `trace[0]` sat at `position_id = 0` (colliding with the preceding `[SEP]` at `0`) and step `k`'s query sat at `position_id = 3k - 1` (for `k >= 1`) rather than `k`. At `HEAD`, [inputs.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/sir_yes_sir/inputs.py#L83-L84) starts `TokenType.OUTPUT` at `position_id = 1`, aligning every step-`k` query token (`[SEP]` at `k = 0`, `o_{k-1}` at `k >= 1`) at `position_id = k` with `x_k` (`position_id = k`).
3. **`halt_accuracy` class-imbalance inflation at `586f44d`**: At `586f44d`, `evaluate_ar` scored `halt_accuracy` *per step* (`halt_ok_count / total_steps`, checking `pred_step[2] == targ_step[2]`), which inflated scores to `(L - 1) / L` (`66.7%` at `L = 3`) for a model that always predicted `FLAG_CONTINUE` — and even `1.0` once the 3-step rhythm was learned, as seen in `2026-11-12-586f44d-sharp-mammal` (`lr = 0.03`, `exact_match = 0.0`, `step_accuracy = 0.01`, `halt_accuracy = 1.0`). At `HEAD`, [evaluation.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/sir_yes_sir/variants/ar/evaluation.py#L113-L125) scores `halt_accuracy` *per problem* (`halt_ok_count / num_problems`).
4. **Missing config controls & provenance flags**: `586f44d` had no `resample_alphabet` flag (always resampled/permuted), no `fixed_d_model` flag (omitted embedding slices shrank `d_model` and required manually passing `d_position = 0` in `NOPE` / `TOKEN_TYPE_NOPE`), and no periodic `eval_every` (`eval.jsonl` did not exist). In addition, 5 of the 8 historical `ar` runs (`climbing-pig`, `live-moth`, `merry-serval`, `sharp-mammal`, `refined-crow`) were recorded with `"git_dirty": true`, and the two Vertex AI runs (`vast-oriole`, `refined-crow`) only contain `config.json` and `vertex.json` (results were never pulled). Marking all 8 runs `OUTDATED` is completely accurate.

- **Claim / question expectation vs actual code**:

- **Concrete trigger & impact on numbers**:
  - Running `./yessir.sh run sir_yes_sir ar --symbol-set decimal --alphabet-size 10 --no-resample-alphabet` evaluates solely on fixed `'0'..'9'` prompts, where pure weight memorisation scores `exact_match = 1.0` indistinguishable from in-context lookup.

A naive induction head that copies the token immediately after `x_k` in the prompt (emitting `[SEP]` on every wrap-around, i.e. `0.0%` wrap-around accuracy) still achieves `step_accuracy = 239 / 240 = 99.58%` and `exact_match = (239 / 240)^3 = 98.76%`, masking total failure on the wrap-around boundary rule unless wrap-around accuracy is tracked separately.

### Finding 2 (Medium — Data Disjointness Trap): `eval_seed != seed` does not prevent 100% verbatim train/eval problem overlap in fixed-alphabet or small-pool regimes

- **Where**: [config.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/sir_yes_sir/config.py#L66-L67) and [#L181-L184](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/sir_yes_sir/config.py#L181-L184), [inputs.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/sir_yes_sir/inputs.py#L150-L175), [run.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/sir_yes_sir/variants/ar/run.py#L24-L39)
- **Expectation vs actual code**:
  - [config.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/sir_yes_sir/config.py#L66-L67) documents: *"4. Disjoint train/eval streams: `eval_seed` must differ from `seed` so evaluation does not replay the training problem stream."*
  - While `seed != eval_seed` ensures two independent `random.Random` PRNG streams in `iter_problems`, both draw `(alphabet, x)` uniformly *with replacement* from the same finite problem space without holding out alphabets or `x` sequences:
    - When `resample_alphabet=False`, the entire universe contains only `B^L` distinct `(alphabet, x)` problems.
    - When `resample_alphabet=True` over a pool of size `M = len(symbol_pool)`, the universe contains `[M! / (M - B)!] * B^L` distinct problems.
- **Concrete input/config & empirical probe**:
  - At the default training budget (`max_steps = 1000`, `batch_size = 64` -> `64,000` training problems drawn from `seed = 42`) and `num_eval_problems = 100` (`eval_seed = 999`), probing `iter_problems` directly yields:
    - Default `symbol_set="all"`, `B = 4, L = 3, resample_alphabet=True` (`240 * 239 * 238 * 237 * 4^3 = 207,085,393,920` problems): **`0 / 100`** eval problems appear in training (`0%` overlap).
    - `symbol_set="decimal"`, `B = 4, L = 3, resample_alphabet=False` (`4^3 = 64` problems): **`100 / 100` (`100%`)** of eval problems appear verbatim in training (~1,000 times each).
    - `symbol_set="decimal"`, `B = 10, L = 3, resample_alphabet=False` (`10^3 = 1,000` problems, cited in `memorisation-vs-in-context.json`): **`100 / 100` (`100%`)** of eval problems appear verbatim in training (~64 times each).
    - `symbol_set="decimal"`, `symbol_pool_size = 4, B = 4, L = 3, resample_alphabet=True` (`4! * 4^3 = 1,536` problems, "Canonical permuted" in [task.md](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/sir_yes_sir/spec/task.md#L37) and restricted-pool small-`B` points in `alphabet-length.json`): **`100 / 100` (`100%`)** of eval problems appear verbatim in training (~41.7 times each).
- **Impact on headline numbers**: In small-universe configurations (`(alphabet_count * B^L) <= max_steps * batch_size`), `eval.json` measures accuracy on problem instances already seen tens to hundreds of times during training rather than unseen instances. Note also (as [test_convergence.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/sir_yes_sir/tests/test_convergence.py#L29-L30) itself points out for `symbol_pool_size = 3`) that at `B = 2` (`alphabet-length.json` lower endpoint), cyclic successor over `{a, b}` maps `a -> b` and `b -> a` under *both* `[a, b]` and `[b, a]`, so `B = 2` never tests alphabet-order sensitivity at all (only unordered 2-symbol membership when `len(symbol_pool) > 2`).

### Finding 3 (Low — Metric Interpretation Subtlety): `evaluate_ar` `step_accuracy` and `halt_accuracy` are teacher-forced, diverging from free-running `greedy_generate_ar` whenever an earlier output token is wrong

- **Where**: [evaluation.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/sir_yes_sir/variants/ar/evaluation.py#L38-L126), [generation.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/sir_yes_sir/variants/ar/generation.py#L17-L68), [task.md](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/sir_yes_sir/spec/task.md#L76-L80), [ar.md](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/sir_yes_sir/spec/variants/ar.md#L32-L38)
- **Expectation vs actual code**:
  - `evaluate_ar` faithfully implements the teacher-forced definitions in [ar.md](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/sir_yes_sir/spec/variants/ar.md#L34-L38); `greedy_generate_ar` is never called in `run.py` or `train_and_evaluate.py`.
  - [task.md](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/sir_yes_sir/spec/task.md#L78) accurately states that teacher-forced `exact_match` equals free-running greedy `exact_match` (in exact arithmetic) because causal masking feeds the identical prefix up to the first mismatch. However, neither spec file warns that **`step_accuracy` and `halt_accuracy` do not share this equivalence**:
    1. If a model emits `EOS` prematurely at `k = 0` (`preds[0] == EOS`), free-running `greedy_generate_ar` halts immediately (`output = [EOS]`, zero symbol predictions), whereas `evaluate_ar` still feeds ground-truth `o_0, ..., o_{L-1}` at indices `P .. P + L - 1` and credits any `preds[k] == o_k` (`k >= 1`) toward `step_accuracy`.
    2. If a model emits a wrong token at `k < L` (for example `[SEP]` or a wrong symbol), free-running decoding appends that wrong token into `TokenType.OUTPUT`, altering subsequent causal attention keys/values (especially in `NOPE` and `TOKEN_TYPE_NOPE` where output step count must be inferred from prefix tokens), whereas `evaluate_ar` evaluates downstream `step_accuracy` and `preds[L] == EOS` (`halt_accuracy`) conditioned on the clean ground-truth prefix `(o_0, ..., o_{L-1})`.
- **Concrete empirical verification**:
  - Training `Transformer(head_type=HeadType.SUBSPACE_LM, n_layers=2, n_heads=4, d_ff=64, d_position=8, d_scratch=16)` for 120 steps (`B = 3, L = 3, symbol_pool=b"012", seed=7`) and comparing `evaluate_ar` against `greedy_generate_ar` across the exact same 100 evaluation problems (`eval_seed = 999`):
    - `exact_match` matched identically in every mode (`0.10` in `nope`, `0.05` in `token_type_nope`, `0.08` in `token_type_pe`).
    - `step_accuracy` differed in every mode (`0.4767` teacher-forced vs `0.4500` free-running in `nope`; `0.4567` vs `0.4133` in `token_type_nope`; `0.5333` vs `0.5033` in `token_type_pe`), and `halt_accuracy` differed by 5 percentage points in `nope` (`0.71` teacher-forced vs `0.76` free-running).
- **Impact**: Headline `exact_match` is unaffected (note also that `yessir/training.py` and `evaluate_ar` run in `float32` without `bfloat16` autocast). Diagnostic `step_accuracy` and `halt_accuracy` (e.g. in [cls-vs-ar.json](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/studies/sir_yes_sir/cls-vs-ar.json#L5) comparing *"halting reliability, and accuracy"*) must be interpreted as teacher-forced next-token metrics rather than free-running rollout metrics when `exact_match < 1.0`.

### Finding 4 (Low — Config, Sinusoidal & CLI Gotchas): Default `eval_every=None` logs no learning curve at default `max_steps=1000`; `resample_alphabet` interacts with `symbol_pool_size=None`; dataset CLI omits symbol-pool flags; `d_position=2` has short sinusoidal period

- **Where**: [config.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/sir_yes_sir/config.py#L75-L107) (`#L187-L193`, `#L235-L240`), [defaults.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/yessir/defaults.py#L9-L14), [dataset.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/sir_yes_sir/variants/ar/dataset.py#L60-L68), [inputs.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/sir_yes_sir/inputs.py#L177-L211), [embedding.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/yessir/embedding.py#L49) and [#L85-L106](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/yessir/embedding.py#L85-L106)
- **Details & concrete triggers**:
  1. **Single-point `eval.jsonl` when `max_steps <= 2500` and `eval_every=None`**: `default_eval_every(max_steps)` returns `max(2500, max_steps // 20)` ([defaults.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/yessir/defaults.py#L12-L14)). Because `SirYesSirConfig.max_steps` defaults to `1000`, leaving `eval_every=None` sets `effective_eval_every = 2500 > 1000`, so [train_and_evaluate.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/sir_yes_sir/train_and_evaluate.py#L89-L107) evaluates **only once, at the final step**. Five `sir_yes_sir` studies ask about *"convergence speed"* or *"sample efficiency"*; runs with `max_steps <= 2500` must pass an explicit `--eval-every <N>` (`0 < N < max_steps`) to record intermediate accuracy checkpoints in `eval.jsonl`.
  2. **Toggling `resample_alphabet` when `symbol_pool_size=None` and `B < len(symbol_set)`**: With `symbol_set="decimal"`, `alphabet_size=4`, and `symbol_pool_size=None` (default), `resample_alphabet=False` slices `symbol_pool[:4] = b"0123"` (4 active token embeddings), whereas `resample_alphabet=True` samples 4-tuples from all 10 bytes `b"0123456789"` (10 active token embeddings). [task.md](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/sir_yes_sir/spec/task.md#L37) documents `symbol_pool_size=B` for "Canonical permuted", so ablations in `memorisation-vs-in-context.json` must explicitly set `symbol_pool_size=B` to hold the active symbol set constant.
  3. **Standalone dataset inspector CLI omits `--symbol-set` and `--symbol-pool-size`**: `add_common_dataset_args` ([inputs.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/sir_yes_sir/inputs.py#L177-L211)) and `_build_from_args` ([dataset.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/sir_yes_sir/variants/ar/dataset.py#L60-L67)) do not wire `symbols` / `symbol_set` / `symbol_pool_size`, so `python -m experiments.sir_yes_sir.variants.ar.dataset` always uses `SymbolSet.ALL` (`0x00..0xEF`).
  4. **Sinusoidal wrap-around at minimal `d_position = 2` (or `L > 1137` at `d_position = 32`)**: `SubspaceEmbedding._sinusoidal_positions` ([embedding.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/yessir/embedding.py#L49) and [embedding.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/yessir/embedding.py#L85-L106)) uses `_SINUSOIDAL_BASE = 256.0`. At default `d_position = 32`, the slowest frequency `256^(-15/16)` has wavelength `~1,137` positions (collision-free across `B <= 240` and moderate `L`, wrapping only if `sequence-length.json` pushes `L > 1,137`). However, if `minimum-model-size.json` sweeps down to `d_position = 2` (`half_pos = 1`), the sole frequency is `1.0 rad/pos` (period `2 * pi ~ 6.283`), giving near-identical encodings at multiples of `2 * pi` (e.g. cosine similarity `0.99984` between `position_id = 0` and `position_id = 44`).

## 3. What Was Read and Verified Correct

### 3.1 Data Generation (`reference.py`, `inputs.py`, `alphabet.py`, `vocab.py`)

- **Ground-truth rule ([reference.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/sir_yes_sir/reference.py#L14-L56))**:
  - Validates `0 <= k < len(x)` (`IndexError`), `len(alphabet) >= 2` (`ValueError`), and membership of `x[k]` in `alphabet` (`ValueError`).
  - Computes `o_k = alphabet[(i + 1) % base]` where `i = alphabet.index(x[k])`, wrapping cleanly from `s_{B-1}` (`i = B - 1`) to `s_0` (`0`).
  - Computes `flag_k = SpecialToken.FLAG_FINISHED` if `k == len(x) - 1` else `SpecialToken.FLAG_CONTINUE` (`build_ar_labelled_example` uses `reference_step(...)[0]` for the `L` symbols and appends `SpecialToken.EOS`).
  - Hand-checked on Spec Example 1 (`alphabet = b"0123", x = b"130" -> o = b"201"`), Spec Example 2 (`alphabet = b"q#7", x = b"#7q7" -> o = b"7q#q"`), minimum boundary (`B = 2, L = 1`, `alphabet = [0x00, 0xEF], x = [0xEF] -> o = [0x00], EOS`), repeated-symbol inputs (`x = b"xyxyx"`), and maximum alphabet size (`B = 240` over `SymbolSet.ALL = 0x00..0xEF`).
- **Vocabulary & special-token disjointness ([vocab.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/yessir/vocab.py#L38-L48), [special_tokens.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/yessir/special_tokens.py#L6-L20), [alphabet.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/yessir/alphabet.py#L22-L70))**:
  - `SYMBOL_RANGE` (`0x00..0xEF`, 240 bytes) and `SPECIAL_RANGE` (`0xF0..0xFF`, 16 bytes: `SEP=0xF0, PAD=0xF1, BOS=0xF2, EOS=0xF3, FLAG_CONTINUE=0xF5, FLAG_FINISHED=0xF6`) partition `0..255` disjointly. `create_alphabet` enforces every symbol is an `int` in `SYMBOL_RANGE` with no duplicates and `2 <= size <= len(pool)`, guaranteeing `o_k` can never collide with `EOS`, `SEP`, `BOS`, or `PAD`.
- **Sampling distributions & RNG isolation ([inputs.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/sir_yes_sir/inputs.py#L150-L175), [dataset.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/sir_yes_sir/variants/ar/dataset.py#L44-L58))**:
  - `iter_problems` creates a local `rng = random.Random(seed)` per iterator, completely isolated from Python's global `random` module and PyTorch's RNG (`seed_everything`).
  - When `resample_alphabet=True`, `rng.sample(pool, B)` draws `B` distinct symbols uniformly without replacement in uniform random order; when `resample_alphabet=False`, `fixed_alphabet = tuple(symbol_pool[:B])` is used verbatim. Each `x_k` (`k = 0 .. L - 1`) is drawn i.i.d. uniformly from `alphabet` via `rng.choice(alphabet)` (allowing repeated symbols in `x`).
  - `train_dataset` (`seed=config.seed`) is consumed through a single persistent `train_stream = iter(train_dataset)` across periodic evaluation chunks ([train_and_evaluate.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/sir_yes_sir/train_and_evaluate.py#L73) and [#L92-L99](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/sir_yes_sir/train_and_evaluate.py#L92-L99)), while `eval_dataset` (`seed=config.eval_seed`) is re-iterated from index 0 on every `evaluate_ar` call so every checkpoint is scored on the exact same `num_eval_problems` problems without perturbing training RNG state.

### 3.2 Tokenisation, Sequence Layout, Causal Shifting, and Train-vs-Eval Parity

- **Exact sequence layout & position coupling ([inputs.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/sir_yes_sir/inputs.py#L65-L131), [inputs.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/sir_yes_sir/variants/ar/inputs.py#L14-L44))**:
  - For prompt prefix `[BOS] s_0..s_{B-1} [SEP] x_0..x_{L-1} [SEP]` of length `P = B + L + 3` and ground-truth symbols `output = (o_0, ..., o_{L-1})`:
    - `example.input_ids` has length `P + L`: `prefix + (o_0, ..., o_{L-1})`. Note `EOS` is **not** appended to `input_ids` (nothing is predicted after `EOS`).
    - `token_type_ids` (in `TOKEN_TYPE_PE` and `TOKEN_TYPE_NOPE`): `0` on `[BOS]` and both `[SEP]`s, `1` on `s_0..s_{B-1}`, `2` on `x_0..x_{L-1}`, and `3` on `o_0..o_{L-1}`.
    - `position_ids` (in `TOKEN_TYPE_PE`): `0` on `[BOS]`, `0..B-1` on `alphabet`, `0` on first `[SEP]`, `0..L-1` on `x`, `0` on second `[SEP]`, and `1..L` on `o_0..o_{L-1}`. Thus at step `k` (`0 <= k < L`), the query position `P - 1 + k` (`[SEP]` at `k = 0`, `o_{k-1}` at `k >= 1`) has `position_id = k`, matching `x_k` (`position_id = k`) with zero offset, while at step `L` (`o_{L-1}`) the query has `position_id = L` (past `0..L-1`), cleanly signalling `EOS`.
  - `labels` has length `P + L`: `(IGNORE_INDEX,) * (P - 1) + (o_0, ..., o_{L-1}, EOS)`.
- **Zero causal / padding / label leakage ([blocks.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/yessir/blocks.py#L102-L115), [batch.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/yessir/batch.py#L59-L154))**:
  - `MultiHeadSelfAttention` calls `F.scaled_dot_product_attention(q, k, v, is_causal=True)` with bias-free projections; `RMSNorm`, `SwiGLUFeedForward`, `SubspaceEmbedding`, and `SubspaceLMHead` all act strictly point-wise per sequence position `(b, t)`.
  - At query index `P - 1 + k` (`0 <= k <= L`), the target `labels[P - 1 + k]` (`o_k` for `k < L`, `EOS` for `k == L`) sits in `input_ids` at index `P + k > P - 1 + k` (or never appears in `input_ids` at all in the case of `EOS`). No target token is ever visible at or before the position predicting it.
  - `collate` right-pads `input_ids` with `SpecialToken.PAD` and `token_type_ids` / `position_ids` with `0`; `collate_labelled` right-pads `labels` with `IGNORE_INDEX = -100`. Under `is_causal=True`, unpadded positions `0 .. length - 1` never attend to padded positions `>= length`. Verified empirically on mixed-length `ar` batches (`batch_size=1` vs `batch_size=4` across all three `EmbeddingMode`s).
- **Train-vs-eval parity**: Training (`SirYesSirArDataset`), teacher-forced evaluation (`evaluate_ar`), and free-running generation (`greedy_generate_ar`) all construct inputs through the same `build_example(..., embedding_mode=...)` function, and `SubspaceEmbedding.forward` ([embedding.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/yessir/embedding.py#L119-L128)) raises `ValueError` immediately if `token_type_ids` or `position_ids` presence (`None` vs tensor) disagrees with `use_token_type` or `d_position > 0`.

### 3.3 Model Architecture, Loss Masking, and End-to-End Config Trace

- **Subspace geometry & `SubspaceLMHead` ([embedding.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/yessir/embedding.py#L52-L156), [heads.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/yessir/heads.py#L21-L53), [model.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/yessir/model.py#L44-L132))**:
  - `SubspaceEmbedding` places the 256-d one-hot `input_ids` slice at coordinates `0..255`, followed by optional 16-d one-hot `token_type_ids`, optional unit-norm float32 sinusoidal `position_ids` (`d_position`), and zero-initialised `d_scratch`.
  - `SubspaceLMHead` applies parameter-free `RMSNorm(d_model, gain=False)` (`||y||_2 = sqrt(d_model)`) and slices `[:, :, :VOCAB_SIZE]`, ignoring `lengths` in `Transformer.forward` ([model.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/yessir/model.py#L130-L131)) because per-position losses and evaluation mask via `labels != IGNORE_INDEX`. Note that in `cls` vs `ar` comparisons (`cls-vs-ar.json`), `Transformer.__init__` instantiates `self.head` before calling `self.reset_parameters()`, so `ParallelClassificationHead`'s two `nn.Linear` layers advance PyTorch's RNG before `reset_parameters()` re-initialises `self.layers` (meaning `cls` and `ar` share identical problem streams for the same `seed`, while trunk initial weights differ as expected for different head architectures).
- **Loss masking & optimiser ([training.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/yessir/training.py#L81-L95) and [#L151-L268](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/yessir/training.py#L151-L268))**:
  - `train_step` sets `model.train()`, zeroes gradients, dispatches `SubspaceLMHead` to `collate_labelled` + `next_token_cross_entropy` (`F.cross_entropy(..., ignore_index=-100)`), and updates via `AdamW(lr=config.lr, betas=(0.9, 0.95), eps=1e-8, weight_decay=0.0)`. Prompt tokens (`0 .. P - 2`) and right-padding (`>= P + L`) contribute exact zero gradient.
- **Complete `SirYesSirConfig` field trace for `ar` ([config.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/sir_yes_sir/config.py#L70-L251), [run.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/sir_yes_sir/variants/ar/run.py#L17-L57), [train_and_evaluate.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/sir_yes_sir/train_and_evaluate.py#L19-L130))**:
  - Every field on `SirYesSirConfig` (`alphabet_size`, `x_length`, `symbol_set`, `symbol_pool_size`, `resample_alphabet`, `embedding_mode`, `n_layers`, `n_heads`, `d_ff`, `d_position`, `d_scratch`, `fixed_d_model`, `max_steps`, `batch_size`, `lr`, `seed`, `eval_every`, `num_eval_problems`, `eval_batch_size`, `eval_seed`) was traced end-to-end into dataset construction, `Transformer` instantiation, `train`, and `evaluate_ar`.
  - **No silent no-ops**:
    - The `cls`-only `supervision` field lives exclusively on the subclass `SirYesSirClsConfig` ([config.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/sir_yes_sir/config.py#L255-L262)), while `AR_VARIANT.config_cls` is `SirYesSirConfig` ([run.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/sir_yes_sir/variants/ar/run.py#L52-L57)); passing `--supervision` on the CLI or in a `--config` JSON file for `ar` raises an immediate `SystemExit` / `TypeError`.
    - When `embedding_mode` is `TOKEN_TYPE_NOPE` or `NOPE`, `d_position` still determines how many dropped dimensions are added to `effective_d_scratch` when `fixed_d_model=True` (`effective_d_scratch = d_scratch + dropped_token_type + d_position`, keeping `d_model = 512` constant as specified in [task.md](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/sir_yes_sir/spec/task.md#L66)); only when *both* `embedding_mode != TOKEN_TYPE_PE` and `fixed_d_model=False` is `d_position` unused (`effective_d_position = 0`, `effective_d_scratch = d_scratch`), which is the intended behaviour of [embedding-mode-width.json](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/studies/sir_yes_sir/embedding-mode-width.json#L5).

### 3.4 Evaluation and Decoding (`variants/ar/evaluation.py`, `variants/ar/generation.py`, `yessir/metrics.py`)

- **Decoding & halting in `greedy_generate_ar` ([generation.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/sir_yes_sir/variants/ar/generation.py#L16-L68))**:
  - Decorated with `@torch.no_grad()` and calls `model.eval()`.
  - Starts from `output = []` after the second `[SEP]` and runs at most `max_tokens = len(x_tokens) + 1 = L + 1` forward passes, feeding back `output` (the model's own greedily chosen `argmax` tokens) at each step and breaking immediately when `next_token == SpecialToken.EOS`.
  - Setting the cap to `L + 1` (rather than `L`, where `L = len(x)` is already visible in the prompt) does **not** leak the halting decision: a model that never emits `EOS` produces `L + 1` non-`EOS` tokens `[o_0, ..., o_{L-1}, extra]` and fails `output == [*expected_symbols, SpecialToken.EOS]`, while a model that emits `EOS` early at `k < L` breaks with `< L + 1` tokens and also fails.
- **Metrics & batch isolation in `evaluate_ar` ([evaluation.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/sir_yes_sir/variants/ar/evaluation.py#L37-L126))**:
  - Decorated with `@torch.no_grad()`, calls `model.eval()`, carries no state across batches, and computes `exact_match = em_count / num_problems`, `step_accuracy = step_ok_count / total_steps` (`L` symbol steps per problem), and `halt_accuracy = halt_ok_count / num_problems` (`all(preds[k] != EOS for k < L) and preds[L] == EOS`), matching [ar.md](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/sir_yes_sir/spec/variants/ar.md#L32-L38) and sharing identical denominators with `cls`.
  - `MetricsLogger` and `write_eval_json` ([metrics.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/yessir/metrics.py#L11-L42), [artefacts.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/yessir/artefacts.py#L151-L164)) sanitise non-finite floats to `null` with `allow_nan=False`.

## 4. What Was Not Checked

1. **GPU (`cuda`) and Apple Silicon (`mps`) kernel execution**: All empirical probes and convergence tests were executed on Linux CPU (`float32`). Determinism warnings or floating-point tie-breaking differences in `F.scaled_dot_product_attention` on CUDA/MPS hardware were not tested live.
2. **Remote Vertex AI container execution (`cloud/vertex/`) and Astro web UI (`web/`)**: `yessir/vertex.py` job-spec construction and `results/sir_yes_sir/*/vertex.json` records were inspected statically, but Docker image builds, GCS FUSE syncing (`entrypoint.sh`), and Astro frontend rendering of `sir_yes_sir` runs/studies were not executed.
3. **Full-scale study sweeps**: Training convergence at large alphabet sizes (`B = 240`), long sequences (`L >> 10`), or minimal 1-layer vs 2-layer `NOPE` / `TOKEN_TYPE_NOPE` configurations (`studies/sir_yes_sir/*.json`) was not empirically benchmarked beyond short CPU probes and the `B = 3, L = 2..3` convergence tests.
