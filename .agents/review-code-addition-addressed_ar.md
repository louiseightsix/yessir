## 1. Executive Summary

- **Primary headline metric (`exact_match` across `id`, `ood_base*`, `extrap_len`, `permuted`)**: **Sound.** No bug, causal leak, label-shift error, train-vs-eval representation mismatch, or silent configuration no-op was found that could produce false `exact_match` or `FirstErrorType` results. Because `Transformer` uses strict causal masking (`is_causal=True`), deterministic greedy `argmax`, and right-padding that cannot be attended to from unpadded positions, the teacher-forced prefix up to and including the earliest error token on any problem is identical to free-running autoregressive generation (up to `float32` GEMM/SDPA batch-shape non-associativity of order `~1e-6`), and `exact_match` checks every supervised trace token (`[IDX]`, `addr(k)`, optional `[S]` / `[C]`, `o_k`, `c_{k+1}`, and terminal `[EOS]`).
- **Secondary headline (`step_accuracy` in `eval.json` / `eval.jsonl`) and diagnostic sub-metrics (`digit_accuracy`, `carry_accuracy`, `halt_accuracy` in `eval_splits.json`)**: **Substantially distorted on failed problems by intra- and inter-column teacher forcing (Finding 1).** Unlike `addressed_step_ar` (where an external harness legitimately supplies `[IDX] addr(k)`), `addressed_ar` claims to test whether the model itself can increment and emit `addr(k)` in its trace and retrieve `(a_k, b_k)` against its own emitted address. However, `evaluate_addressed_ar` feeds the **oracle ground-truth `trace`** into `batch.input_ids` and `score_predictions` ignores `syntax_ok` when computing `step_accuracy`, `digit_accuracy`, and `carry_accuracy`. Consequently, a model whose address counter completely fails at unseen lengths (`L = 21..40` in `extrap_len`) — getting 0% of columns `20..39` right and never halting in free-running rollout — can still record **`extrap_len_step_accuracy = 1.0` (`100%`)** in `eval.json` and `halt_accuracy = 1.0` in `eval_splits.json`.
- **Latent / secondary issues**:
  - Free-running `rollout_addressed_ar` assigns `token_type_ids` and `position_ids` via a hardcoded oracle column-width schedule (`build_addressed_ar_example`) that desynchronises if the model emits a wrong-length address under `TOKEN_TYPE_PE` / `TOKEN_TYPE_NOPE`, and `parse_addressed_ar_trace` marks traces containing special tokens (`[SEP]`, `[PAD]`, `[BOS]`) in address/value slots as `is_well_formed=True` (**Finding 2**).
  - Unnormalised token-level cross-entropy (`next_token_cross_entropy`) weights a `B = 2, L = 20` trace `~26x–28x` more heavily than a `B = 10, L = 1` trace within multi-base batches, and `addressed_ar` trains on `~14x` more column transitions (`~20x–30x` more supervised tokens) per optimiser step than harnessed variants (`cls`, `step_ar`, `addressed_step_ar`) at equal `batch_size` and `max_steps` (**Finding 3**).
  - `halt_accuracy` in `evaluate_addressed_ar` treats non-`[IDX]` syntax mistakes at column boundaries `k >= 1` as halting errors (unlike at `k = 0` and unlike `evaluate_ar`), and `task.md` references nonexistent `bfloat16` autocast (**Finding 4**).
  - `AdditionConfig._split_seed` can collide across consecutive `eval_ood_bases` when `seed == eval_seed + 1000 + b` (**Finding 5**).

## 2. Findings (Suspected Bugs, Spec Mismatches, and Correctness Risks)

### Finding 1 (High — Moves `step_accuracy` in `eval.json` and diagnostic metrics in `eval_splits.json`): Teacher-forced evaluation injects oracle `addr(k)` intra-column and oracle `c_k` inter-column while `step_accuracy` ignores `syntax_ok`

- **Where**:
  - [evaluation.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/addition/variants/addressed_ar/evaluation.py#L71-L165) (`evaluate_addressed_ar`)
  - [evaluation.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/addition/evaluation.py#L193-L208) (`score_predictions`)
  - [train_and_evaluate.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/addition/train_and_evaluate.py#L174-L183) (writes `{prefix}step_accuracy` alongside `{prefix}exact_match` to `eval.json` and `eval.jsonl`)
  - Contrast with [reviewer-code.md](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/.agents/reviewer-code.md#L33-L34), [embedding-modes-length.json](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/studies/addition/embedding-modes-length.json#L1-L8), and [variants-comparison.json](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/studies/addition/variants-comparison.json#L1-L8).
- **Expectation vs. actual behaviour**:
  - [task.md](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/addition/spec/task.md#L143-L150) and [addressed_ar.md](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/addition/spec/variants/addressed_ar.md#L95-L103) deliberately specify teacher-forced evaluation across all six variants, while [reviewer-code.md](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/.agents/reviewer-code.md#L33-L34) explicitly asks whether evaluation decoding is genuinely autoregressive without access to the reference trajectory after problem setup, and whether `step_accuracy` and diagnostic metrics mean what the study claims imply.
  - `rollout_addressed_ar` ([generation.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/addition/variants/addressed_ar/generation.py#L92-L158)) is never called during `run_addition_addressed_ar`. Instead, `evaluate_addressed_ar` constructs `ex = build_addressed_ar_example(prob.alphabet, prob.a, prob.b, trace, ...)` using the **oracle `reference_add` trace** ([evaluation.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/addition/variants/addressed_ar/evaluation.py#L72-L88)) and reads `argmax` predictions at all `len(trace) + 1` positions in one pass.
  - For **`exact_match`** and **`FirstErrorType`**, teacher forcing is valid because causal masking guarantees the input prefix up to the first wrong prediction equals greedy rollout.
  - For **`step_accuracy`**, **`digit_accuracy`**, **`carry_accuracy`**, and **`halt_accuracy`**, two distinct reference leaks occur:
    1. **Intra-column oracle `addr(k)` leakage + `syntax_ok`-blind step scoring (unique to `addressed_ar`)**: In `addressed_ar`, the model is supposed to generate `[IDX] addr(k)` itself and then attend to `(a_k, b_k)` by matching its emitted `addr(k)` against operand addresses ([addressed_ar.md](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/addition/spec/variants/addressed_ar.md#L7)). Under teacher forcing, even when the model predicts the wrong `addr(k)` (`header_ok = False`), the positions predicting `o_k`, `c_{k+1}`, and `after_col_pred` (`[IDX]` / `[EOS]`) immediately condition on the **oracle `addr(k)`** in `batch.input_ids`. Furthermore, `score_predictions` ([evaluation.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/addition/evaluation.py#L193-L205)) computes `digit_ok = pred.sum_digit == step.sum_digit` and `both_ok = digit_ok and (pred.carry_out == step.carry_out)` without checking `pred.syntax_ok`!
    2. **Inter-column carry and halting recovery**: A carry error at column `0` of a long carry chain (e.g. `999...9 + 1`), or a premature `[EOS]` at column `0`, is overwritten in `batch.input_ids` by the oracle's `c_1 .. c_K` and oracle continuation tokens for columns `1 .. K`.
- **Concrete input that triggers it (verified empirically via Python probe)**:
  - Take the exact length-extrapolation failure mode targeted by [embedding-modes-length.json](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/studies/addition/embedding-modes-length.json#L5) and [addition.md](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/.agents/addition.md#L32-L33): base `B = 10` (`alphabet = b"0123456789"`), `L_a = L_b = 22` (`a = b"1"*20 + b"4"*2`, `b = b"1"*20 + b"5"*2`), and a model trained on `L <= 20` whose address counter caps the MSD digit at `'1'` (emitting `('0', '1')` and `('1', '1')` at `k = 20, 21` instead of `('0', '2')` and `('1', '2')`), while accurately looking up `(a_j, b_j)` and halting whenever the preceding in-context address matches `addr(21) = ('1', '2')`.
  - **Free-running `rollout_addressed_ar` result**: gets columns `20..21` wrong (`20 / 22 = 90.91%` step accuracy; `20 / 40 = 50.0%` on `L = 40` with distinct digits) and **never halts** (`well_formed = False`, runs to `max_tokens = 106`).
  - **`evaluate_addressed_ar` result**: `EvalMetrics(exact_match=0.0, step_accuracy=1.0, digit_accuracy=1.0, carry_accuracy=None, halt_accuracy=1.0, syntax_malformation=1.0)` — reporting **`100%` `step_accuracy`**, **`100%` `digit_accuracy`**, and **`100%` `halt_accuracy`**! (Indeed, [test_addressed_ar.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/addition/tests/test_addressed_ar.py#L740-L751) explicitly asserts `step_accuracy == 1.0` and `halt_accuracy == 1.0` when `addr(0)` is wrong.)
- **Could this move headline numbers?**
  - **Does NOT move `exact_match`** (the first key in `eval.json`, used as `headlineMetric` in `web/`).
  - **DOES move `step_accuracy` (including `extrap_len_step_accuracy` and `ood_base*_step_accuracy` in `eval.json` and `eval.jsonl`) as well as `digit_accuracy`, `carry_accuracy`, and `halt_accuracy` in `eval_splits.json`.** Specifically, in `addressed_ar` length-extrapolation studies (`L = 21..40`), `extrap_len_step_accuracy` measures whether the model can add *given oracle-injected addresses and carries* (i.e. `addressed_step_ar` behaviour), masking failures of the model's own address counter and carry propagation.

### Finding 2 (Medium — Latent defect in `generation.py` / `inputs.py` free-running helpers): `build_addressed_ar_example` hardcodes oracle column widths during `rollout_addressed_ar`, and `parse_addressed_ar_trace` accepts special tokens in address/value slots

- **Where**:
  - [inputs.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/addition/variants/addressed_ar/inputs.py#L86-L116) (`build_addressed_ar_example`)
  - [generation.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/addition/variants/addressed_ar/generation.py#L35-L89) (`parse_addressed_ar_trace`)
  - [generation.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/addition/variants/addressed_ar/generation.py#L130-L157) (`rollout_addressed_ar`)
- **Expectation vs. actual behaviour**:
  1. **Coordinate desynchronisation in `rollout_addressed_ar` under `TOKEN_TYPE_PE` / `TOKEN_TYPE_NOPE`**: When `rollout_addressed_ar` rebuilds the `Example` at each step from `trace`, `build_addressed_ar_example` never inspects `trace` for `AdditionToken.IDX` (`0xFB`) or `[S]` / `[C]` (`0xF8` / `0xF9`). Instead, it steps `slot` modulo `col_len = 1 + len(encode_address(k, alphabet_tokens)) + body_len` ([inputs.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/addition/variants/addressed_ar/inputs.py#L90-L115)). If the model emits a wrong-width address (for instance, emitting a 1-symbol address instead of a 2-symbol address at `k = B`), every subsequent token in `trace` receives shifted `token_type_ids` and `position_ids` — even assigning `TokenType.CARRY` or `TokenType.ADDRESS` to literal `[IDX]` tokens!
  2. **Over-permissive `parse_addressed_ar_trace`**: Its docstring ([generation.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/addition/variants/addressed_ar/generation.py#L43-L45)) states that `is_well_formed` is `True` iff `emitted` consists of well-formed `[IDX] addr(k) ...` segments followed by a single `[EOS]`. In code, `parse_addressed_ar_trace` splits on `IDX`, checks only `len(seg) >= 1 + body_len` (and `pred_q0 == q_0, pred_q1 == q_1` when `use_query_tokens=True`), and never checks that `addr` or `(sum_digit, carry_out)` are symbol tokens in `0x00..0xEF` (rather than `[SEP]`, `[PAD]`, `[BOS]`, `[F]`, or stray `[S]` / `[C]` tokens).
- **Concrete input that triggers it (verified empirically)**:
  - Calling `parse_addressed_ar_trace([AdditionToken.IDX, SpecialToken.SEP, SpecialToken.PAD, SpecialToken.BOS, SpecialToken.EOS])` returns `([AddressedTraceStep(address=(240,), sum_digit=241, carry_out=242)], True)` (`is_well_formed == True`).
  - Calling `build_addressed_ar_example(b"01", b"111", b"1", [251, 48, 48, 49, 251, 49, 48, 49, 251, 49, 48, 49, 251, 48, 49, 48, 48])` (where column `k = 2` emitted a 1-bit address `49` instead of 2 bits) assigns `token_type_ids = [0, 6, 5, 4, 0, 6, 5, 4, 0, 6, 6, 5, 4, 0, 6, 6, 5]`, tagging the fourth `IDX` (`251` at trace offset `12`) as `TokenType.CARRY` (`4`) instead of `TokenType.SPECIAL` (`0`).
- **Could this move headline numbers?**
  - **No** for `train_and_evaluate` artefacts (`eval.json`, `eval_splits.json`, `eval.jsonl`), which do not call `rollout_addressed_ar` or `parse_addressed_ar_trace` (and any rollout reaching a width error has already failed `exact_match`). It affects post-error continuation in `rollout_addressed_ar` (for `TOKEN_TYPE_PE` / `TOKEN_TYPE_NOPE`, not `NOPE`) and any caller relying on `parse_addressed_ar_trace`'s `is_well_formed` flag.

### Finding 3 (Low / Experimental-Design Subtlety — Affects training dynamics & cross-variant step comparisons): Within-batch token-count loss skew (`~26x–28x` toward long base-2 traces) and `~14x–30x` transition/token budget gap vs. harnessed variants

- **Where**:
  - [dataset.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/addition/variants/addressed_ar/dataset.py#L70-L90) (`AdditionAddressedArDataset.__iter__`)
  - [run.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/addition/variants/addressed_ar/run.py#L27-L56) (`run_addition_addressed_ar`)
  - [training.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/yessir/training.py#L151-L184) (`next_token_cross_entropy`)
  - Relevant to [variants-comparison.json](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/studies/addition/variants-comparison.json#L5) and [addition.md](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/.agents/addition.md#L36).
- **Expectation vs. actual behaviour**:
  - The implementation matches [addressed_ar.md](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/addition/spec/variants/addressed_ar.md#L88-L93), which specifies full-trace supervision per example via `next_token_cross_entropy`. Two quantitative consequences should be kept in mind when interpreting runs:
    1. **Within-batch length and base skew**: `next_token_cross_entropy` averages uniformly over all non-`-100` tokens in the collated batch (`F.cross_entropy(..., ignore_index=IGNORE_INDEX)`). On default training settings (`bases = (2, 4, 8, 10)`, `L = 1..20`), a `B = 2, L = 20` overflow problem has `181` supervised tokens (`use_query_tokens=True`) or `139` (`use_query_tokens=False`), whereas a `B = 10, L = 1` non-overflow problem has `7` or `5` supervised tokens (`25.9x` or `27.8x` gradient weight ratio), and `[IDX] + addr(k)` tokens comprise `55%`–`73%` of all supervised loss positions in base 2.
    2. **Cross-variant budget per optimiser step**: Whereas `step_fork` explicitly adjusts `train_batch_size = 3 * config.batch_size` to preserve 1-transition-per-problem parity with `cls`, `step_ar`, and `addressed_step_ar` (`64` column transitions = `192` supervised target tokens per step), `ar` and `addressed_ar` train on `64` **complete problems** (`~875` column transitions and `~4,000–6,000` supervised tokens) per step.
- **Could this move headline numbers?**
  - Not a code bug, so recorded numbers are faithful to the spec; however, comparing "convergence speed" by optimiser `step` in [variants-comparison.json](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/studies/addition/variants-comparison.json#L5) gives `addressed_ar` `~14x` more column transitions per step than `addressed_step_ar`.

### Finding 4 (Low — Cross-variant metric asymmetry & minor spec drift): `halt_accuracy` boundary asymmetry in `evaluate_addressed_ar` vs. `evaluate_ar`, and `bfloat16` wording in `task.md`

- **Where**:
  - [evaluation.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/addition/variants/addressed_ar/evaluation.py#L135-L152) vs. [evaluation.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/addition/variants/ar/evaluation.py#L124-L129)
  - [task.md](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/addition/spec/task.md#L145) vs. [training.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/yessir/training.py#L200-L268)
- **Details**:
  1. In `evaluate_addressed_ar` (matching [addressed_ar.md](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/addition/spec/variants/addressed_ar.md#L103)), `halt_ok` checks `no_premature_eos and (after_col_pred == expected_after)` where `expected_after` is `AdditionToken.IDX` for `k < last_k` and `SpecialToken.EOS` for `k == last_k`. As a result, predicting a non-`[IDX]`, non-`[EOS]` token (e.g. `ord('q')`) before column `0` (`preds[0]`) yields `halt_accuracy = 1.0`, whereas the exact same non-`[IDX]`, non-`[EOS]` error before column `1` (`preds[E_0]`) yields `halt_accuracy = 0.0` ([test_addressed_ar.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/addition/tests/test_addressed_ar.py#L753-L777)). By contrast, `evaluate_ar` checks only `no_premature_eos` for `k < last_k` ([evaluation.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/addition/variants/ar/evaluation.py#L124-L129)), so non-`[S]` syntax errors between columns in `ar` (`use_query_tokens=True`) leave `halt_accuracy = 1.0`.
  2. Note also that if premature `[EOS]` is predicted at a value slot (`o_k` or `c_{k+1}`), `classify_first_error` classifies the problem under `out_of_alphabet` rather than `syntax_malformation` ([evaluation.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/addition/evaluation.py#L132-L153), [test_addressed_ar.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/addition/tests/test_addressed_ar.py#L779-L789)), matching the within-column check order in [task.md](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/addition/spec/task.md#L153-L158).
  3. Minor spec text drift: [task.md](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/addition/spec/task.md#L145) attributes batch-shape `argmax` flips to *"under `bfloat16` autocast"*, whereas the entire live `yessir` codebase trains and evaluates strictly in `float32` with no `autocast` (`float32` GEMM/SDPA non-associativity across batch shapes can still flip near-tied logits at `~1e-6`).

### Finding 5 (Low — Edge-case RNG seed collision): `AdditionConfig._split_seed` collides across consecutive OOD bases when `seed == eval_seed + 1000 + b`

- **Where**: [config.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/addition/config.py#L353-L387) (`AdditionConfig._split_seed`, `AdditionConfig.eval_splits`)
- **Expectation vs. actual behaviour**:
  - `_split_seed(offset)` computes `candidate = self.eval_seed + offset` and returns `candidate + 1 if candidate == self.seed else candidate`. It checks for collision only against `self.seed`, not against other splits' candidate seeds.
- **Concrete config that triggers it (verified empirically)**:
  - `AdditionAddressedArConfig(bases="2,8,10", eval_ood_bases="3,4", eval_seed=999, seed=2002)` produces `ood_base3.seed == 2003` and `ood_base4.seed == 2003`. Both OOD splits instantiate `random.Random(2003)` and reuse the same sequence of random alphabet symbol draws and operand draws.
- **Could this move headline numbers?**
  - **No** on default settings (`seed=42`, `eval_seed=999`, `eval_ood_bases="3,12"` give distinct seeds `999, 2002, 2011, 2999, 3999`), and `bases=(3,)` vs `bases=(4,)` remain base-disjoint even if triggered.

## 3. What Was Read and Verified Correct

### 3.1 Data generation (`reference.py`, `inputs.py`, `alphabet.py`)
- **Ground-truth addition oracle** ([reference.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/addition/reference.py#L38-L186)):
  - Verified `_validate_inputs`, `_compute_step`, `reference_step`, and `reference_add` line by line and against hand-worked cases:
    - Spec Example 1 (`987 + 25 = 1012` in base 10, `a = b"789"`, `b = b"52"`): `4` steps (`k = 0..3`), carries `0 -> 1 -> 1 -> 1 -> 0`, sum `b"2101"`, `FLAG_CONTINUE` on `k = 0..2` and `FLAG_FINISHED` on `k = 3`.
    - Spec Example 2 (`alphabet = b"q#7"`, `a = b"7#"` (`5`), `b = b"#"` (`1`)): `2` steps (`k = 0..1`), sum `b"q7"` (`6`), carry `#` then `q`, `FLAG_FINISHED` on `k = 1`.
    - Single-digit `0 + 0` (`K = 0`, `1` step, immediate `FLAG_FINISHED`), single-digit overflow `1 + 1` in base 2 (`K = 1`, `2` steps, `o_0 = s_0, o_1 = s_1`), full carry ripple `(B^L - 1) + 1` overflowing into `k = L`, unequal operand lengths (`k >= L_a` or `k >= L_b` substituting `0`), MSD zeros preserved up to `max(L_a, L_b) - 1`, minimum base `B = 2`, and maximum base `B = 240` (`SymbolSet.ALL`).
- **Base-`B` column address encoding** ([inputs.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/addition/inputs.py#L115-L138)):
  - `encode_address(j, alphabet)` rejects `bool`, negative ints, and `len(alphabet) < 2`.
  - Returns `(alphabet[0],)` for `j == 0`, and unpadded variable-width `LSD -> MSD` base-`B` digits `(alphabet[d_0], alphabet[d_1], ...)` for `j > 0`: `1` token for `0 <= j < B`, `2` tokens for `B <= j < B^2`, up to `6` tokens for `j = 32..40` in base 2. Uses the problem's own `alphabet` (including permuted canonical alphabets, so in `alphabet = b"10"`, `addr(0) = (ord('1'),)` and `addr(2) = (ord('1'), ord('0'))`).
- **Sampling distributions & zero-preamble constraints** ([inputs.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/addition/inputs.py#L344-L495), [alphabet.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/yessir/alphabet.py#L22-L70)):
  - `sample_problem` draws `base` uniformly from `bases`, chooses `SymbolSet.HEX[:base]` with probability `p_prior` or `B` distinct bytes from `symbol_pool` (`0x00..0xEF`) otherwise, and on `permuted_canonical=True` redraws `create_alphabet(SymbolSet.HEX[:base], rng=rng)` until `alphabet != canonical` (so `B = 2` is deterministically `('1', '0')`, never leaking ascending canonical order).
  - When `include_alphabet=False` (zero-preamble mode), `eff_min = max(min_length, base)` ([inputs.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/addition/inputs.py#L431)) and `validate_problem` ([inputs.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/addition/inputs.py#L160-L166)) enforce `L_a >= B` and `L_b >= B`, ensuring all single-digit addresses `addr(0) .. addr(B - 1)` (`s_0 .. s_{B-1}`) appear in order in both operands.
  - Carry-chain injection ([inputs.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/addition/inputs.py#L441-L450)): when `min(L_a, L_b) >= 2`, with probability `p_chain`, samples `m` in `1 .. n_shared - 1` and `i` in `0 .. n_shared - 1 - m`, setting `a_vals[i] + b_vals[i] >= B` and `a_vals[j] + b_vals[j] == B - 1` for `j` in `i + 1 .. i + m`. Indices stay strictly within `0 .. min(L_a, L_b) - 1`.
- **Train/eval split disjointness & RNG streams** ([config.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/addition/config.py#L167-L186), [config.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/addition/config.py#L358-L416), [train_and_evaluate.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/addition/train_and_evaluate.py#L119-L179)):
  - `eval_seed == seed` is rejected (`__post_init__`). Any base present in `bases` is stripped from `eval_ood_bases`, so `ood_base*` splits strictly use unseen bases; `extrap_len` uses `max_length + 1 .. eval_extrap_max_length` (`21..40`), strictly disjoint from training lengths `1..20`; `permuted` uses `permuted_canonical=True` (`alphabet != canonical`), strictly disjoint from ascending canonical alphabets.
  - Periodic evaluations in `train_and_evaluate` instantiate a fresh `iter_problems(..., seed=split.seed)` per split (`random.Random(split.seed)`), never touching the persistent `train_stream = iter(train_dataset)` (`random.Random(config.seed)`) or PyTorch's global RNG.

### 3.2 Tokenisation, sequence layout, and causal / padding isolation (`inputs.py`, `batch.py`, `blocks.py`, `embedding.py`)
- **Vocabulary & special tokens** ([vocab.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/yessir/vocab.py#L38-L48), [special_tokens.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/yessir/special_tokens.py#L6-L20), [inputs.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/addition/inputs.py#L20-L43)):
  - Symbols occupy `0x00..0xEF` (`240` bytes); shared `SpecialToken`s occupy `0xF0..0xF6` (`[SEP]=0xF0`, `[PAD]=0xF1`, `[BOS]=0xF2`, `[EOS]=0xF3`); `AdditionToken`s occupy `0xF8..0xFB` (`[S]=0xF8`, `[C]=0xF9`, `[F]=0xFA`, `[IDX]=0xFB`). Zero overlap between symbol bytes and control/query/delimiter tokens.
- **Exact sequence, `token_type_ids`, and `position_ids` layout** ([inputs.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/addition/inputs.py#L259-L326), [inputs.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/addition/variants/addressed_ar/inputs.py#L23-L117)):
  - Matches [addressed_ar.md](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/addition/spec/variants/addressed_ar.md#L35-L66) across all `2 x 2 x 2 x 3 = 24` combinations of `(step_order, use_query_tokens, include_alphabet, embedding_mode)`:
    - Prefix: `[BOS]` `(SPECIAL, 0)`, optional `s_0..s_{B-1}` `(ALPHABET, 0..B-1)` + `[SEP]` `(SPECIAL, 0)`, operand `a` entries `[IDX] addr(j) a_j` at `(SPECIAL, j)`, `(ADDRESS, j)*len(addr(j))`, `(OPERAND_A, j)`, `[SEP]` `(SPECIAL, 0)`, operand `b` entries `[IDX] addr(j) b_j` at `(SPECIAL, j)`, `(ADDRESS, j)*len(addr(j))`, `(OPERAND_B, j)`, `[SEP]` `(SPECIAL, 0)`.
    - Trace column `k`: `[IDX]` `(SPECIAL, k)`, `addr(k)` `(ADDRESS, k)*len(addr(k))`, optional `[S]` / `[C]` `(SPECIAL, k)`, `o_k` `(OUTPUT, k)`, and `c_{k+1}` `(CARRY, k + 1)` (under both `sum_first` and `carry_first`, `c_{k+1}` sits at `position_id = k + 1`, aligning with `a_{k+1}`, `b_{k+1}`, and `addr(k + 1)` of the next column!).
- **Target shifting, loss masking, and causal / right-padding invariance** ([inputs.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/addition/variants/addressed_ar/inputs.py#L120-L156), [batch.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/yessir/batch.py#L59-L154), [blocks.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/yessir/blocks.py#L102-L115)):
  - `labels = (IGNORE_INDEX,) * (P - 1) + (*trace, EOS)` has length `P + len(trace) == len(example)`.
  - Every supervised position `P - 1 + t` (`0 <= t <= len(trace)`) predicts `targets[t]` using only `input_ids[0 .. P - 1 + t]` under `F.scaled_dot_product_attention(..., is_causal=True)`. No target token is visible at or before the position that predicts it; all prompt positions `0 .. P - 2` and all right-padded positions `>= length` have `labels == -100` (`IGNORE_INDEX`).
  - In `addressed_ar`, `[IDX]`, `addr(k)`, optional `[S]` / `[C]`, `o_k`, `c_{k+1}`, and `[EOS]` are **all** supervised in training (`build_addressed_ar_labelled_example`) and **all** generated autoregressively by the model in `rollout_addressed_ar` (zero harness injection in the trace).

### 3.3 Model, positional bounds on `L = 40` in base 2, training, and end-to-end config trace
- **Sequence length & positional bounds on `L = 40` in base 2** ([embedding.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/yessir/embedding.py#L49-L106), [model.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/yessir/model.py#L115-L132), [generation.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/addition/variants/addressed_ar/generation.py#L116-L123)):
  - Verified empirically: for `B = 2`, `L_a = L_b = 40` with carry overflow (`41` columns, `6`-bit addresses at `k = 32..40`), total sequence length is **`829` tokens** (`use_query_tokens=False`, `308` supervised targets) or **`911` tokens** (`use_query_tokens=True`, `390` supervised targets).
  - Neither `SubspaceEmbedding`, `MultiHeadSelfAttention`, nor `Transformer` has any hardcoded `max_seq_len` buffer or lookup table (`_sinusoidal_positions` computes `float32` sinusoids dynamically for arbitrary `(B, T)`). Moreover, `position_ids` in `addressed_ar` index **columns** rather than raw tokens, so at `L = 40` in base 2 `max(position_ids) == 41` (well below `_SINUSOIDAL_BASE = 256.0`'s half-wavelength of `~569` positions).
  - In `rollout_addressed_ar`, `max_tokens` sums `1 + len(encode_address(k, alphabet_tokens)) + body_len` over `k in range(max(len(a), len(b)) + 1)` plus `1` for `[EOS]` (`390` tokens for `B = 2, L = 40, use_query_tokens=True`), never truncating a valid overflow trace.
  - Under `@torch.no_grad()`, `eval_batch_size = 64` at `T = 911` (`n_heads = 8`, `float32`) requires at most `~1.7 GB` for an explicit `(64, 8, 911, 911)` attention score tensor (and far less under fused SDPA kernels), so default `eval_batch_size = 64` stays well within standard GPU memory.
- **End-to-end config trace** ([config.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/addition/config.py#L84-L527), [run.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/addition/variants/addressed_ar/run.py#L20-L56), [train_and_evaluate.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/addition/train_and_evaluate.py#L48-L211)):
  - Every field on `AdditionAddressedArConfig` was traced from CLI/JSON parsing (`ExperimentConfig` / `add_config_args`) through `__post_init__` into `AdditionAddressedArDataset`, `Transformer`, `train`, and `evaluate_addressed_ar`: `bases`, `min_length`, `max_length`, `symbol_set`, `symbol_pool_size`, `p_prior`, `p_chain`, `embedding_mode`, `n_layers`, `n_heads`, `d_ff`, `d_position`, `d_scratch`, `fixed_d_model`, `max_steps`, `batch_size`, `lr`, `log_every`, `seed`, `eval_every`, `num_eval_problems`, `eval_batch_size`, `eval_seed`, `eval_p_chain`, `eval_ood_bases`, `eval_extrap_max_length`, `eval_permuted_canonical`, `step_order`, `use_query_tokens`, and `include_alphabet`.
  - Step-sampling parameters (`p_terminal`, `all_steps`) live on `AdditionStepConfig` only and are **not** inherited by `AdditionArConfig` / `AdditionAddressedArConfig`, avoiding any silent no-op fields on `addressed_ar`.
  - `fixed_d_model=True` accurately expands `effective_d_scratch` (`208 -> 240 -> 256`) when dropping `d_position=32` (`TOKEN_TYPE_NOPE`) and `MAX_TOKEN_TYPES=16` (`NOPE`), keeping `d_model = 512` invariant across embedding ablations, and `SubspaceEmbedding.forward` enforces exact presence/absence agreement of `token_type_ids` and `position_ids` between `Example` and `Transformer`.
