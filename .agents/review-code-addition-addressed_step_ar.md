- **Experiment**: `addition`
- **Variant**: `addressed_step_ar` (`ADDRESSED_STEP_AR_VARIANT`)
- **Audited `HEAD` commit**: `bc2244b9fc121bbbfaf96302e51e7e0082083cac`
- **Recorded run commits in `results/addition/`**: None (`results/addition/` does not exist yet at this commit; all 13 study files in `studies/addition/*.json` are in `"draft"` status with `"runs": []`).
- **Overall verdict**: **Implementation matches specification; zero headline `exact_match`-corrupting bugs found.** The reference oracle, base-`B` column address encoder (`encode_address`), zero-preamble length invariant (`include_alphabet=False`), section-coupled `token_type_ids` and `position_ids`, causal target shifting and `IGNORE_INDEX` loss masking across all four `(step_order, use_query_tokens)` combinations, config plumbing across all 30 fields of `AdditionAddressedStepArConfig`, and greedy rollout (`rollout_addressed_step_ar`) are mathematically sound and consistent between training and evaluation. However, four **metric-interpretation, default-configuration, distributional, and systems subtleties** warrant explicit attention before running or interpreting studies—most notably that teacher-forced evaluation in `evaluate_addressed_step_ar` (while provably identical to free-running rollout on `exact_match` and `FirstErrorType` in exact arithmetic) inflates post-error `step_accuracy` (recorded in `eval.json`) and introduces a `CARRY_FIRST` vs `SUM_FIRST` conditioning asymmetry in `digit_accuracy` and `halt_accuracy`, and that `AdditionAddressedStepArConfig` defaults to `embedding_mode=EmbeddingMode.TOKEN_TYPE_PE` (injecting sinusoidal `position_ids = j` alongside `[IDX] addr(j)` unless explicitly overridden).

## 1. Findings (Suspected Bugs, Spec Mismatches, and Correctness Risks)

Zero bugs were found that corrupt `exact_match`, leak future targets across the causal mask, mis-encode base-`B` addresses, or render any `config.py` field a silent no-op. Below are the four substantive methodological, configuration, and edge-case risks identified during the audit, ordered by impact on reported numbers and study claims.

### Finding 1 (Medium — Metric & Claim Interpretation Risk): Teacher-forced evaluation in `evaluate_addressed_step_ar` matches free-running rollout on `exact_match` and `FirstErrorType`, but diverges on `step_accuracy` (in `eval.json`) and creates a `CARRY_FIRST` conditioning asymmetry in `digit_accuracy` and `halt_accuracy`

- **Where**:
  - [evaluation.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/addition/variants/addressed_step_ar/evaluation.py#L60-L109) (`evaluate_addressed_step_ar`)
  - [run.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/addition/variants/addressed_step_ar/run.py#L44-L50) (`run_addition_addressed_step_ar`)
  - [evaluation.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/addition/evaluation.py#L193-L208) (`score_predictions`)
  - Contrast with [generation.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/addition/variants/addressed_step_ar/generation.py#L48-L85) (`rollout_addressed_step_ar`), [task.md](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/addition/spec/task.md#L143-L150), and [.agents/reviewer-code.md](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/.agents/reviewer-code.md#L33-L34).
- **Spec vs code behaviour**:
  - Both [task.md](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/addition/spec/task.md#L143-L145) and [addressed_step_ar.md](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/addition/spec/variants/addressed_step_ar.md#L93) explicitly specify teacher-forced evaluation across all `K + 1` ground-truth columns, while `rollout_addressed_step_ar` in [generation.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/addition/variants/addressed_step_ar/generation.py#L17-L86) is never called by `run.py` or `train_and_evaluate.py` (it is exercised only in unit and convergence tests).
  - **Why `exact_match` and `FirstErrorType` are unaffected**: Because `MultiHeadSelfAttention` uses strict causal masking (`is_causal=True`), `RMSNorm` and `SwiGLUFeedForward` act position-wise, and `SubspaceLMHead` normalises per position, every supervised position up to and including the earliest prediction mismatch on a problem sees the exact same token, token-type, and position prefix in `evaluate_addressed_step_ar` as in step-by-step greedy `rollout_addressed_step_ar`. Thus teacher-forced `exact_match` and all four `FirstErrorType` rates equal free-running greedy rollout in exact arithmetic (verified empirically across all 8 combinations of `step_order`, `use_query_tokens`, and `include_alphabet`).
  - **Outer-loop divergence on `step_accuracy` (in `eval.json`), `digit_accuracy`, `carry_accuracy`, and `halt_accuracy`**: In [evaluation.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/addition/variants/addressed_step_ar/evaluation.py#L64-L79), column `k + 1` always receives the ground-truth `step.carry_in` (`c_{k+1}`) and ground-truth `sum_digits[:k+1]`, and evaluation always scores `K + 1` oracle columns regardless of `pred_flag`.
  - **Within-step divergence and `step_order` asymmetry on `digit_accuracy` and `halt_accuracy`**: Within step `k`, [evaluation.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/addition/variants/addressed_step_ar/evaluation.py#L65-L78) appends ground-truth `(t_0, t_1)` before reading `preds_t0`, `preds_t1`, and `preds_flag`. Single-step `step_accuracy` (`both_ok = (pred_t0 == t_0) and (pred_t1 == t_1)`) is algebraically invariant to within-step teacher forcing (if `pred_t0 == t_0`, the teacher-forced `t_0` matches `pred_t0`; if `pred_t0 != t_0`, `both_ok` is already `False`). However:
    1. `digit_accuracy` only tests `pred_o == o_k` ([evaluation.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/addition/evaluation.py#L195-L198)). Under `StepOrder.SUM_FIRST`, `o_k` is `t_0` (unconditioned on `c_{k+1}`). Under `StepOrder.CARRY_FIRST`, `o_k` is `t_1` and is predicted conditioned on the **oracle** outgoing carry `t_0 = c_{k+1}` rather than the model's own predicted `pred_c`!
    2. `halt_accuracy` tests `pred_flag == int(step.flag)` ([evaluation.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/addition/variants/addressed_step_ar/evaluation.py#L126)), read at the third supervised slot after teacher-forced `(t_0, t_1)`. At column `k = max(L_a, L_b) - 1`, halting depends on whether `c_{k+1} == s_0`; `pred_flag` always sees the **oracle** `c_{k+1}` in its causal context even when `pred_c` was wrong.
- **Concrete trigger (verified via Python probe)**:
  - *Within-step trigger (single-column problem, zero outer-loop steps)*: On `alphabet = b"0123456789"`, `a = b"4"`, `b = b"5"` (`o_0 = '9'`, `c_1 = '0'`, `flag_0 = FLAG_FINISHED`) under `StepOrder.CARRY_FIRST` and `use_query_tokens=True`, a conditional-subtraction model that mispredicts `pred_c = '1'` at `[C]` and computes `o_0` and `flag_0` consistently from the carry token in context emits `('0', '1', FLAG_CONTINUE)` twice in `rollout_addressed_step_ar` (`0%` digit accuracy, fails to halt), yet `evaluate_addressed_step_ar` reports `digit_accuracy = 1.0` and `halt_accuracy = 1.0` (with `exact_match = 0.0`, `step_accuracy = 0.0`).
  - *Outer-loop trigger*: On `a = b"999"`, `b = b"1"` in base 10 (`K + 1 = 4` carry-active steps), mispredicting only `c_1 = '0'` at `k = 0` gives `step_accuracy = 0.75` and `carry_accuracy = 0.75` in `evaluate_addressed_step_ar`, whereas free-running rollout feeds `carry = '0'` into `k = 1`, producing `'899'` (`0/4` steps right).
- **Impact on headline / diagnostic numbers**:
  - **Headline `exact_match` (`eval.json` first key)**: **No impact** (matches free-running rollout in exact arithmetic).
  - **Secondary headline `step_accuracy` (`eval.json`) & diagnostic metrics (`eval_splits.json`)**: `step_accuracy` measures per-column transition accuracy given clean oracle state `(c_k, o_0..o_{k-1})`, not multi-step rollout survival; and in [step-order.json](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/studies/addition/step-order.json#L1-L8), `digit_accuracy` favours `CARRY_FIRST` over `SUM_FIRST` on columns where carry prediction fails because `CARRY_FIRST` receives the oracle `c_{k+1}` before predicting `o_k`.

### Finding 3 (Low — Distributional Confounders in Zero-Preamble & Length Extrapolation): Per-base `min_length` clamping under `include_alphabet=False` shifts eval length distributions and PRNG streams, and `L <= 20` leaves high-order address digits unseen

- **Where**:
  - [inputs.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/addition/inputs.py#L115-L139) (`encode_address`) and [inputs.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/addition/inputs.py#L431-L436) (`sample_problem`)
  - [config.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/addition/config.py#L488-L506) (`_validate_zero_preamble_lengths`)
- **Concrete triggers & behaviour**:
  1. **Per-base length clamping (`include_alphabet=False`)**: When `min_length = 1, max_length = 20, bases = "2,4,8,10", eval_ood_bases = "3,12"`, setting `include_alphabet=False` clamps `eff_min = max(min_length, base)` per sampled problem (`inputs.py:431`). Consequently:
     - `ood_base12` evaluates operand lengths `12..20` (mean `16.0`) under `include_alphabet=False`, versus `1..20` (mean `10.5`) under `include_alphabet=True`.
     - Because `len_a` and `len_b` change, `sample_problem` consumes a different number of `rng.randrange(base)` calls per problem, so the `id`, `ood_base3`, and `ood_base12` problem streams are not problem-for-problem matched against an `include_alphabet=True` baseline unless `min_length >= max(bases, eval_ood_bases)` (e.g. `min_length = 12`).
     - Boundary case: if `max_length == b` (e.g. `AdditionAddressedStepArConfig(bases="2,8", max_length=12, eval_ood_bases="12", include_alphabet=False)`), `_validate_zero_preamble_lengths` accepts the config and `ood_base12` silently evaluates only fixed-length `len_a = len_b = 12` problems (`eff_min == max_length == 12`).
  2. **Unseen leading address digits on `extrap_len` (`L = 21..40`)**: Confirming the confounder noted in [.agents/addition.md](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/.agents/addition.md#L33) and [embedding-modes-length.json](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/studies/addition/embedding-modes-length.json#L5), `encode_address(j, alphabet)` emits minimal-width `LSD -> MSD` base-`B` numerals. With `max_length = 20`, operand addresses only span `j in 0..19` (and overflow `k = 20`):
     - In `B = 10`, 2-digit operand addresses (`10..19`) only ever have `s_1` as their MSD (`s_2` appears only in carry/hint headers on rare `k = 20` overflow steps, never on operand digits, and `s_3` at `30..39` is completely unseen).
     - In `B = 12`, 2-digit addresses (`12..20`) only ever have `s_1` as their MSD (`s_2` and `s_3` at `24..40` are completely unseen).
     - In `B = 2`, training sees 1- to 5-symbol addresses (`0..20`), whereas `extrap_len` (`32..40`) introduces 6-symbol addresses.
- **Impact on headline numbers**: Neither behaviour is a code bug (both follow [addressed_step_ar.md](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/addition/spec/variants/addressed_step_ar.md#L19) and [addressed_ar.md](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/addition/spec/variants/addressed_ar.md#L13-L17)), but `zero-preamble.json` comparisons with `min_length = 1` test `include_alphabet=False` on strictly longer average problems than `include_alphabet=True`.

## 2. What Was Read and Verified Correct

### 2.1 Data Generation & Reference Oracle (`reference.py`, `inputs.py`, `alphabet.py`)

- **Ground-truth column addition ([reference.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/addition/reference.py#L69-L186))**:
  - Verified `_validate_inputs`, `_compute_step`, `reference_step`, and `reference_add` by hand against Spec Examples 1 (`987 + 25 = 1012` in base 10: 4 steps `k = 0..3`, overflow at `k = 3` emitting `o_3 = '1', c_4 = '0', flag_3 = FLAG_FINISHED`) and 2 (`5 + 1 = 6` in base-3 `['q', '#', '7']`: 2 steps `k = 0..1`), single-column `0 + 0` (`K = 0`, 1 step, immediate `FLAG_FINISHED`), single-column `1 + 1` in base 2 (`K = 1`, 2 steps, carry overflow), MSD leading zeros (`b"500" + b"2"` -> 3 steps `b"700"`), full carry ripples `(B^L - 1) + 1`, and maximum base `B = 240` (`SymbolSet.ALL`).
  - Verified `total = v_a + v_b + v_c <= 2 * B - 1`, so `total // B` is strictly in `{0, 1}` (`s_0` or `s_1`), and at overflow column `k = max(L_a, L_b)` (`v_a = v_b = 0, v_c = 1`), `total = 1 < B` since `B >= 2`, guaranteeing `carry_out == s_0` and termination at `k <= max(L_a, L_b)`.
- **Base-`B` address construction (`[IDX] addr(j)` in [inputs.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/addition/inputs.py#L115-L139) & [inputs.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/addition/inputs.py#L259-L275))**:
  - **Endianness**: Strictly `LSD -> MSD` (`digits.append(alphabet[remaining % base]); remaining //= base`), matching operand digit order and [addressed_ar.md](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/addition/spec/variants/addressed_ar.md#L13-L17). Checked `j = 0 -> (s_0,)`, `0 <= j < B -> (s_j,)`, `j = 24` in base 10 `-> ('4', '2')`, `j = 5` in base 2 `-> (s_1, s_0, s_1)`, and `j = 40` across `B in {2, 3, 4, 8, 10, 12, 16}`.
  - **Width & non-leakage of `L`**: Minimal width (`1` token for `j == 0`, `floor(log_B(j)) + 1` tokens for `j > 0`) with no MSD zero-padding. `encode_address(j, alphabet)` takes only `(j, alphabet)`—never `L_a`, `L_b`, or `K`—so address widths never leak total operand or output length. Multi-digit addresses are delimited on the left by `[IDX]` (`0xFB`) and followed by the single entry value digit before the next `[IDX]` or `[SEP]`, making address boundaries unambiguous even in `EmbeddingMode.NOPE`.
  - **In-context alphabet mapping & zero-preamble solvability**: `encode_address` indexes `alphabet[d]`, so under permuted canonical alphabets (e.g. `b"2031"` where `'2' = 0, '0' = 1`) and random byte alphabets, address digits use the prompt's own symbol assignment rather than ASCII numerals. Under `include_alphabet=False`, `validate_problem` ([inputs.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/addition/inputs.py#L160-L166)) and `sample_problem` ([inputs.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/addition/inputs.py#L431)) enforce `L_a >= B` and `L_b >= B`. Consequently, entries `j = 0 .. B - 1` of both operands always contain exactly the `B` single-token addresses `[IDX] s_0 a_0 ... [IDX] s_{B-1} a_{B-1}` in ascending value order (and any entry `j >= B` has `>= 2` address tokens), uniquely determining both `B` and `(s_0, ..., s_{B-1})` even at the boundary `L_a = L_b = B`.
- **Sampling distributions, train/eval disjointness, and RNG separation ([inputs.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/addition/inputs.py#L403-L549), [config.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/addition/config.py#L353-L416))**:
  - **Alphabets**: `permuted_canonical=True` redraws `create_alphabet(SymbolSet.HEX[:base], rng=rng)` in a `while alphabet == canonical:` loop ([inputs.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/addition/inputs.py#L424-L425)), ensuring permuted splits never emit ascending canonical order (and in `B = 2` always emit `b"10"`). `p_prior` mixes ascending `SymbolSet.HEX[:base]` with uniform `rng.sample(symbol_pool, base)`.
  - **Carry-chain injection**: Verified index bounds in [inputs.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/addition/inputs.py#L441-L450): when `n_shared = min(len_a, len_b) >= 2`, `m in 1 .. n_shared - 1` and `i in 0 .. n_shared - 1 - m`, with `a_vals[i] + b_vals[i] >= base` (guaranteed carry generation) and `a_vals[j] + b_vals[j] == base - 1` for `j in i + 1 .. i + m` (guaranteed `m`-step propagation).
  - **Step sampling & PRNG decoupling**: `iter_problem_steps` ([inputs.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/addition/inputs.py#L534-L548)) uses a dedicated `random.Random(f"{seed}:step")` instance independent of `iter_problems(seed=seed)`, selecting `k = K` with probability `p_terminal` and `randint(0, K)` otherwise. Changing `p_terminal`, `all_steps`, or `eval_every` leaves the underlying problem stream untouched.
  - **Split disjointness & seed separation**: `AdditionConfig.__post_init__` rejects `eval_seed == seed` ([config.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/addition/config.py#L289-L292)) and strips any training base from `eval_ood_bases` ([config.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/addition/config.py#L173-L184)). `_split_seed` derives non-colliding seeds across `id` (`+0`), `ood_base{b}` (`+1000 + b` for `2 <= b <= 240`), `extrap_len` (`+2000`), and `permuted` (`+3000`), bumping by `+1` if any candidate equals `self.seed`. `ood_base*` (unseen `B`), `extrap_len` (`L_a, L_b >= max_length + 1`), and `permuted` (non-ascending `HEX[:B]` vs ascending `HEX[:B]` when `symbol_set=ALL`) are structurally disjoint from training.

### 2.2 Tokenisation, Coordinates, Causal Masking, and Positional Capacity

- **Vocabulary partitioning ([vocab.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/yessir/vocab.py#L38-L48), [special_tokens.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/yessir/special_tokens.py#L6-L20), [inputs.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/addition/inputs.py#L20-L43))**:
  - `SYMBOL_RANGE` (`0x00..0xEF`), `SHARED_SPECIAL_RANGE` (`0xF0..0xF7`: `[SEP]`, `[PAD]`, `[BOS]`, `[EOS]`, `[FLAG_CONTINUE]`, `[FLAG_FINISHED]`), and `EXPERIMENT_SPECIAL_RANGE` (`0xF8..0xFB`: `[S]`, `[C]`, `[F]`, `[IDX]`) are strictly disjoint, preventing any symbol-vs-control collision.
- **`token_type_ids`, `position_ids`, and `EmbeddingMode` ([addressed_step_ar/inputs.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/addition/variants/addressed_step_ar/inputs.py#L31-L117), [inputs.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/addition/inputs.py#L171-L203))**:
  - Every section matches the table in [addressed_step_ar.md](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/addition/spec/variants/addressed_step_ar.md#L31-L49): `[BOS]` and all `[SEP]` delimiters have `(SPECIAL, 0)`; `alphabet` has `(ALPHABET, 0..B-1)` when `include_alphabet=True` and is cleanly omitted along with its trailing `[SEP]` when `include_alphabet=False`; operand `a`, operand `b`, and output history entries `j` assign `position_id = j` across `[IDX]` (`SPECIAL`), all digits of `addr(j)` (`ADDRESS`), and the value digit (`OPERAND_A` / `OPERAND_B` / `OUTPUT`); incoming carry `[IDX] addr(k) c_k` and step-`k` hint `[IDX] addr(k)` (plus `[S]`, `[C]`, `[F]` when `use_query_tokens=True` and `o_k`) sit at `position_id = k`; and outgoing carry `c_{k+1}` sits at `(CARRY, k + 1)` under both `SUM_FIRST` and `CARRY_FIRST`.
  - `make_example` validates equal lengths of `input_ids`, `token_type_ids`, and `position_ids` before dropping `position_ids` (`TOKEN_TYPE_NOPE`) or both (`NOPE`).
  - **100% train / eval / rollout parity**: `AdditionAddressedStepArDataset`, `evaluate_addressed_step_ar`, and `rollout_addressed_step_ar` all call the exact same `build_addressed_step_ar_example` helper and `collate` right-padder.
- **Sinusoidal positional capacity on long binary sequences ([embedding.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/yessir/embedding.py#L49-L106))**:
  - `SubspaceEmbedding._sinusoidal_positions` computes `sin` / `cos` analytically in `float32` on arbitrary `(B, T)` tensors with no fixed `max_seq_len` lookup table, so `802`-token sequences (`B = 2, L = 40, k = 40`) cannot trigger an out-of-bounds error. Moreover, because `position_ids` encode column indices rather than raw token offsets, the maximum `position_id` on an `802`-token `L = 40` sequence is `41` (or `B - 1 <= 239` in the alphabet preamble), well inside `_SINUSOIDAL_BASE = 256.0`'s half-wavelength (`~569` positions).
- **Causal masking & right-padding isolation ([batch.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/yessir/batch.py#L59-L99), [blocks.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/yessir/blocks.py#L102-L115))**:
  - `collate` right-pads `input_ids` with `SpecialToken.PAD` and `token_type_ids` / `position_ids` with `0`. Because `F.scaled_dot_product_attention(..., is_causal=True)` blocks position `t < length` from attending to `t' > t`, trailing `PAD` positions have zero mathematical effect on real token representations (confirmed via `batch_size=1` vs `batch_size=64` probe on a 3-layer `Transformer`).

    - `use_query_tokens=True`: sequence length `P_k + 5` ending in `(Q_0, t_0, Q_1, t_1, Q_2)`; `labels` has `-100` everywhere except `t_0` at `P_k` (`Q_0`), `t_1` at `P_k + 2` (`Q_1`), and `flag_k` at `P_k + 4` (`Q_2`). ### 2.4 Evaluation, Free-Running Rollout, Error Taxonomy, and Artefacts

- **Outer-loop rollout ([generation.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/addition/variants/addressed_step_ar/generation.py#L16-L86))**:
  - Starts with `carry = alphabet_tokens[0]` (`s_0`) and `output = []`, calls `model.eval()` under `@torch.no_grad()`, decodes `t_0, t_1, flag_k` via 3 sequential `argmax` forward passes per step, unpacks `(o_k, c_next)` via `unpack_step_order`, halts immediately on `flag_k == SpecialToken.FLAG_FINISHED`, and otherwise updates `carry = c_next` and `output.append(o_k)` (even if `c_next` or `o_k` is an out-of-alphabet or special token).
  - Step-`k` addresses `addr(k)` (`k = len(output)`) are formatted solely from `(k, alphabet_tokens)` with zero access to `reference_add` / `reference_step`; `max(len(a_tokens), len(b_tokens)) + 1` is used solely as the outer loop safety cap when `FLAG_FINISHED` is never emitted.
- **Metrics & 4-way error taxonomy ([evaluation.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/addition/evaluation.py#L70-L222), [metrics.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/yessir/metrics.py#L11-L42))**:
  - Verified `exact_match`, `step_accuracy`, `digit_accuracy`, `carry_accuracy` (`None` when `carry_active_steps == 0`), `halt_accuracy` (per-problem conjunction `all(p.halt_ok for p in preds)`), and partition of unity `exact_match + out_of_alphabet + prior_intrusion + procedural_error + syntax_malformation == 1.0`.
  - Verified `prior_intrusion` fires only when `is_canonical_symbol_set(alphabet)` holds (`set(alphabet) == set(SymbolSet.HEX[:B])`), `pred_val != exp_val`, `pred_val in alphabet_set`, and `pred_val == SymbolSet.HEX[alphabet.index(exp_val)]` (which is impossible on ascending canonical alphabets because `HEX[digit_value] == exp_val`).
  - Verified `model.eval()` is called before evaluation ([evaluation.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/experiments/addition/variants/addressed_step_ar/evaluation.py#L81)) and `model.train()` is restored on every `train_step` ([training.py](file:///atelier/code/workshop/yessir/yessir/training.py#L225)), with no state leaked across batches or periodic `eval_every` checkpoints.
